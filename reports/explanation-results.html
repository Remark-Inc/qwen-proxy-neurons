Interpretation limits

  • Exploratory explanation of one fixed checkpoint and one fixed intervention. No neuron has a verified one-concept semantic label.
  • Prior failure traces are reused as explanation data; they are not new independent validation.
  • Natural conditional failure prefixes here are SEC scenarios; synthetic contrasts vary hosts. The 60 controlled contrasts include some shared prefixes with different alternative continuations, not 60 independent tasks.
  • A conditional log-probability change does not establish tool-initiation control. Natural generation is reported separately.
  • A restore effect is conditional on the other eleven being ablated; a null effect does not establish irrelevance or dispensability.
  • Pair interactions are nonadditivity of output scores, not evidence of direct wiring between the neurons; normalization and probability nonlinearities can contribute.
  • Multiple comparisons are exploratory; rankings and pair interactions have no confirmatory significance claims.
  • Generation jobs reuse the same seed schedule for their own control arms. Do not pool these controls as independent replications. GPU numerical differences can produce different trajectories across jobs even with the same seeds.
  • Both source and destination tokenization and prefix choice affect the measured contrast. Absolute branch log-probabilities are retained.
  • The hostname score includes a standalone slash token, which differs from /pro in a /proxy_temp_file path. A no-slash sensitivity analysis is reported separately; scored token-prefix probabilities are not sums over every possible string tokenization.
  • Timing windows are predictor positions. Ablating prompt positions includes processing the supplied initial call and tool response.
  • Full-sequence teacher forcing may have numerical differences from cached decoding. The smoke test checked hook identity and equivalence, not bitwise replay of all cached trajectories.
  • Restoring late decision coordinates tests one mediation route, not the entire causal circuit.
  • Broad helpfulness benchmarks and pruning remain deferred. Explicit-proxy tasks test specificity, not endorsement of unsolicited proxy use.
  • Resource estimates exclude startup, failed-container overhead and storage; the conservative reservation ledger includes the failed packaging attempt.

Methodological references: Zhang & Nanda, activation-patching best practices; Heimersheim & Nanda, interpreting activation patching.
